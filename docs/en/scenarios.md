## Accepted limits

- **Assessment of the facts**: the tool says a covenant is unenforceable when a safeguard is missing, but it does not judge whether 12 months is in fact reasonable, whether the employer's belief was reasonable or whether dismissal fell within the band of reasonable responses. Those are for the tribunal or court: the scenario sets them out in the commentary.
- **Constructive dismissal, prise d'acte**: the verdict only depends on the presence of a linked repudiatory (or serious) breach; the scenario explains that the tribunal decides.
- **Annual figures**: English-law calculations use the figures from 6 April 2026 as parameters (see [Checking rules](checking-rules.md#annual-figures-and-reform-parameters)); French-law calculations follow the statutory rules only, not more favourable collective agreements.

With the same building blocks: **discrimination** and reasonable adjustments, **whistleblowing** (automatically unfair dismissal, uncapped award), **capability** and ill-health dismissals, **collective consultation** on redundancies, **zero-hours** contracts and the Employment Rights Act 2025, **holiday** and working time, **TUPE** (as free items and custom conditions); under French law, harassment, remote working, unfitness, economic dismissal, protected employees.
